Two-player games you can play online with a friend on different devices

You are on your laptop, they are on their phone across town. Every game below runs in a browser tab: one of you starts a room, sends the link or the room code, and the other one is in. Free, no account, no download, no ads.

Games for two, on two devices

  • Farmyshoot

    Capture the flag in first person, with farm animals: five maps, four modes, laptop or phone.

    2 to 16 players, in any mix of people and bots. Invite by room code, no account.

  • Farmy Kart

    Kart racing with eight farm-animal drivers, six circuits in two cups, drift turbos and cowpats.

    Race friends; computer drivers fill the rest of the eight-kart grid. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Tiles

    A word-tile game on the full fifteen by fifteen board, with the real hundred-tile bag and premium squares.

    Two to four players on one board, or you against the computer. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Crosswords

    Four word games on one page - Farmy Five, Farmy Hive, Farmy Herds and Farmy Furrows - with big letters and no timer.

    Up to four people working the same puzzle together. Invite by room code, no account.

  • Farmy Chess

    Complete chess: castling, en passant, promotion and every draw. Big high-contrast pieces, no clock.

    Two players, or you against a computer that plays at your level. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Checkers

    English draughts: twelve sheep against twelve cows, compulsory takes, multiple jumps and crowned kings.

    Two players, or you against a computer that plays at your level. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Ludo

    Four farm teams, a six to come out and an exact roll to finish. Every team has its own shape as well as a colour.

    Up to four players; the computer takes any team nobody took. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Moon Life

    A cozy farming game on a tiny moon: grow an orchard, make jam, and buy the land from a cat in a hat.

    You and a friend, who visits your moon and helps. Invite by room code, no account.

Which one for which evening

  • Twenty quiet minutes and a bit of thinking: Farmy Chess is complete chess, with castling, en passant, promotion and every draw, in big high-contrast pieces. Farmy Checkers is English draughts, sheep against cows, and quicker to learn.
  • Chatting on a call while you play: Farmy Ludo. Luck keeps it friendly, and with two of you the computer runs the other two teams.
  • You both like words: Farmy Tiles if you want to compete on a full fifteen by fifteen board, or Farmy Crosswords if you would rather solve the same puzzle together. It is co-operative, not a race, and there is no timer.
  • Something loud: Farmyshoot is capture the flag in first person, and bots fill both teams when there are only two of you. Farmy Kart puts you both on an eight-kart grid with computer drivers in the rest.
  • Something slow: in Farmy Moon Life you read out your moon's six-character code and your friend comes to visit and help with the harvest.

How to play with a friend on another computer or phone

  1. Open the game's page here and press Play. Nothing downloads; the game starts in the tab.
  2. Start a room. Chess, Checkers, Ludo, Tiles and Kart give you a link to send by text, email or chat. Farmyshoot, the word games and Moon Life give you a short room code you can read out loud.
  3. Your friend opens the link, or types the code, in any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a laptop, a Chromebook, a tablet or a phone.

There is no account at either end and no game server in the middle: the two browsers connect directly. If a room never connects, the usual cause is a very locked-down network that blocks browser-to-browser connections, typically a work VPN. Switching one device to a phone hotspot is the fix.

Questions

Can two people play these games on different computers or phones?

Yes. One of you starts a room and sends the link or reads out the room code; the other opens it in their own browser, on a laptop, a Chromebook or a phone. The two browsers connect to each other directly.

Do we need to sign up or download anything?

No. Every game runs in the browser tab. There is no account to make, nothing to install, and no ads.

What if my friend is not online right now?

Chess, checkers, ludo and Farmy Tiles all have a computer opponent, so you can play alone and invite a person later.
