The magazine

What we are making, what is new in it, and how to be good at it.

Magestican Studios makes small games that open in the tab you are already in. No download, no launcher, no account, no waiting — you follow a link and you are playing, on whatever you happen to be holding. There are twelve you can play right now, one we are still fixing and one on the bench.

This is where we write about them: what is in an update and what it changed, how the games are meant to be played, what we are trying to do with them and what we have decided not to do. If you want to know whether a game is for you before you spend a click on it, start here.

Everything we publish is playable for free in a browser. The quickest way to find out if any of it is any good is to open one.
