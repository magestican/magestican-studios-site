Board games to play online with friends, free and without an account

The board is on the table and your friend is in another city. Four classic board games, each playable in a browser tab: start a room, send the link, and you are both looking at the same board on your own computers or phones. No sign-up, no download, no ads, and no clock.

  • Join from a link
  • Different computers
  • No clock
  • No ads

The board games

  • Farmy Tiles

    A word-tile game on the full fifteen by fifteen board, with the real hundred-tile bag and premium squares.

    Two to four players on one board, or you against the computer. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Chess

    Complete chess: castling, en passant, promotion and every draw. Big high-contrast pieces, no clock.

    Two players, or you against a computer that plays at your level. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Checkers

    English draughts: twelve sheep against twelve cows, compulsory takes, multiple jumps and crowned kings.

    Two players, or you against a computer that plays at your level. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Ludo

    Four farm teams, a six to come out and an exact roll to finish. Every team has its own shape as well as a colour.

    Up to four players; the computer takes any team nobody took. Invite by link, no account.

Which board for which friend

  • The one who takes it seriously: Farmy Chess is the full game, with castling, en passant, promotion and every draw. It is two players, and if nobody is around the computer plays at your level.
  • The one who wants something quicker: Farmy Checkers is English draughts: twelve sheep against twelve cows, takes are compulsory, multiple jumps chain, and a piece that reaches the far side is crowned.
  • The family group chat: Farmy Ludo takes up to four. You need a six to come out and an exact roll to get home, which is exactly as unfair as it was on the kitchen table. Every team has a shape as well as a colour, so nobody needs to tell red from orange.
  • The word person: Farmy Tiles is a crossword tile game on the full fifteen by fifteen board, with the real hundred-tile bag, premium squares and the fifty-point bonus for using all seven tiles. Two to four players, or you against the computer.

How to set up a game with a friend on another computer

  1. Open the game's page and press Play. The board appears in the tab; nothing installs.
  2. Choose to play with a friend and start a room. You get a link.
  3. Send the link however you usually talk: text, email, a group chat, the chat box of a video call.
  4. Your friend opens it in their browser - laptop, Chromebook, tablet or phone - and takes a seat.

Neither of you makes an account. The two browsers connect directly, so there is no server holding your game. The pieces are big and clear, which helps on a phone and helps anybody whose eyes are not what they were.

Questions

Can I play chess online with a friend without signing up?

Yes. Open Farmy Chess, start a room and send the link. Your friend opens it in their browser and you are on the same board. Neither of you makes an account.

How many people can play Ludo online together?

Up to four, each on their own device, joining from one link. The computer takes any team nobody took, so two or three players still get a full game.

Is there a clock or a timer?

No. None of the four board games has a clock, so nobody loses on time.
