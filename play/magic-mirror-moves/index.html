    Privacy

    The camera picture in Magic Mirror Moves stays on this device.

    The whole game runs in this browser, and there is no server code. Each camera frame is used once to find your child's arms, legs and head, and then it is dropped.

    The picture is never uploaded, recorded or saved. The game has no tracking, analytics, cookies or ads.

    The game keeps one thing: the best score for each game mode, as a number, on this device. The "Forget my scores" button clears it.

    The camera switches off when the round ends or you close the page.
