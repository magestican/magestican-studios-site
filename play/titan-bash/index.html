    How to play
    • Fire shoots the nearest rival ahead. Boost dashes; dash into a rival to slam.
    • Everything heats your Titan. Past the Red Line you hit 25 % harder. At 100 you stall.
    • Stalled? Tap Boost when the ring meets the mark: a Perfect Vent = Overdrive.
    • Empty a rival's Armour, or knock them off the edge, to ring their Bell. Most Bells wins.

    Player 1: W A S D move, J fire, K boost, L ability. Player 2: arrows, 1 fire, 2 boost, 3 ability.
