How to play

First team to capture 5 flags wins. Ties go to a 10-second anagram duel.

  • Move — WASD + mouse (desktop) · left thumb joystick + right thumb look (mobile)
  • Jump — Space / JUMP button. Press again mid-air to double-jump (your animal yells!)
  • Shoot — click / FIRE. Keys 1-3 or tap to switch weapons
  • 🚩 Grab the enemy flag, run it back to your barn while it glows blue over your head
  • 🐔 Chicken slingshot — grab it on the centre hill; your next shot obliterates someone
  • 🥩 Steaks — shoot the 4 floating steaks at the map edges; collect 5 to throw a poison steak
  • 🌾 Hay bales — walk inside one to hide; you can see out, they can't see in
