Congawise

Level
Code -

Take a card from the stock or the pile, then throw one. Make escaleras (3 or more in a row of one suit) and piernas (3 or 4 of one number); cut when what is left over adds up to 5 or less. Over 100 points you are out, and the last one in wins. A conga - all 7 in one escalera - wins at once.
