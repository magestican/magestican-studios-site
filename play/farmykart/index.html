Controls

Keyboard — ↑ or W accelerate, ↓ or S brake and reverse, ←→ steer, Shift drift, Space use item, Q look back, Esc pause.
Gamepad — right trigger accelerate, left stick steer, shoulder buttons drift, X or Y for items.
Touch — hold anywhere on the right to accelerate, slide your left thumb to steer (slide it down to brake), and the bottom-right corner drifts. The button left of it uses your item.
