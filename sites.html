Magestican Studios sites

Magestican Studios is the trading name of Ultra Task Pty Ltd, in Australia. Magestican Games is this site. These are the other sites we run.

Reference and data

  • Gold Price In What gold is worth today, in your country and your currency.
  • KabuFacts Public data on the Japanese stock market: financials, dividends and valuation for 100 Nikkei 225 companies, in English and Japanese.
  • Plain Almanac Sunrise, sunset, solar noon, day length and moon phase for 291 cities in 31 languages, computed from the NOAA solar algorithm.
  • SkyPollen A live 3D map of pollen, bushfire smoke and satellite fire hotspots on today's forecast wind. Modelled estimates with official links.
  • Navigation Atlas Live map of the rivers and canals of Europe and South America: draughts, locks, bridge clearances, ports and water levels, with sources.
  • World Tension Map A world map of active conflicts and a tension score for every country, with climate, hunger, jobs, prices, refugees and 47 stock markets.
  • Good News of the Day True, recent, well-sourced good news every morning: science, health, nature, kindness and milestones, each credited to its source.

Games and music

  • Work Chill Out Free music to work and study to, with no ads and no sign-up: original lofi, chill J-pop and 90s game songs in one loop, with a focus timer.
  • Magestican Music A music agency that develops, produces and generates bands of any genre. First act: Super Five on Magestican Records.
  • Velvet Crystal The four-member virtual K-pop girl group from Magestican Records: debut singles with karaoke lyrics.

  • Hire a Cleaner House cleaning and home services by local Ultra Taskers, across major Australian cities.
  • Cleaning Jobs AU Cleaning and handyman jobs on the Gold Coast and in Brisbane: pick the jobs you want and see what each one pays before you apply.
