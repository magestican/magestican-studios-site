Ringtitans — a giant robot party game for up to eight

Ringtitans is a giant robot party game that runs in your browser: up to eight friends in one room each pilot a Titan on one shared screen, and try to ring each other's Bells. In its world, cities under glass domes fight their quarrels out in an arena, the Bell Trials, so that they never go to war, and every pilot flies for one of eight Houses. Nobody dies here: when a Titan's armour runs out, its Bell rings and the pilot floats away on a chute. It is free, with nothing to download and no account.

Getting everyone in

Open the game on a television, a laptop or a monitor that everyone can see. A room code and a QR code appear on it. Each friend scans the code with a phone, and the phone becomes their controller, with a stick and three buttons. Two more people can share the keyboard, and gamepads work too. Any seat nobody takes is filled by a CPU pilot, so a round always has eight Titans.

Three buttons and the Heat

Fire shoots, aimed for you at the nearest rival ahead. Boost dashes when you tap it and runs when you hold it. Ability is the special move of your Titan.

Shooting and dashing heat your Titan. At 70 Heat and over you are on the Red Line: your shots and slams hit a quarter harder and knock rivals further. At 100 your Titan stalls for over a second and cannot move. While it is stalled a ring closes round it. Tap Boost as the ring meets the mark and you make a Perfect Vent: it ends the stall at once, empties your Heat and gives you Overdrive, so you move faster and your shots cost no Heat. Good pilots learn to run hot on purpose.

Slams and Clangs

Dash straight into a rival and you slam them, which shoves them hard. When two Titans dash into each other at the same moment, that is a Clang: both bounce back, and the first pilot to press Fire gets a free hit at point-blank range.

Bells and the Bounty

Knock out all of a rival's armour and you ring their Bell: that scores you a Bell, and they drop back in a pod three seconds later with a short shield. On an arena with open edges you can also knock a Titan off the side for a ring-out. The pilot leading by two Bells glows gold, and ringing that pilot's Bell is worth two. Lumen crystals fall onto the floor as well, and picking up three gives a short Lumen Overdrive.

The four Titans

  • Lancer, the all-rounder. Its Blade Lunge dashes far and ends in a sword sweep.
  • Bulwark, the heavy one. Its Aegis raises a front shield that sends bolts back at the shooter.
  • Wisp, fast and light. Its Afterimage blinks it away and leaves a decoy behind.
  • Mortar, the artillery. Its Seed Barrage drops a ring of shells round the spot you aim at.

Three arenas and the Storm Hour

The Bell Ring has open edges on every side, so ring-outs are common. Shatterfield is walled and full of crystal pillars that block shots and shatter when a Titan dashes through. Tidewall Causeway is three bridges over the sea, and every 30 seconds a wave sweeps one of them, after a three-second glow to warn you.

A round lasts two and a half minutes. In the last 40 seconds the Storm Hour begins: a wall of static creeps inward, every Bell is worth two, and new music starts. Then the Sleeper, a giant arm and head, rises from a glowing crack and swings at the gold pilot, or at the nearest Titan when nobody glows. Six hits on its glowing core send it back down.

Modes and a Trial Night

Bell Brawl is everyone for themselves: most Bells wins the round. Team Trial splits the Houses into two, three or four teams. A Trial Night runs for three, four or five rounds, across the arenas in turn, and ends on a podium for the best pilots.

Help for new pilots

Each player can turn on Rookie for themselves, which widens the auto-aim and the window for a Perfect Vent, and shows a leaf badge by their name. Before the first round there is a warm-up with grey targets to practise on. A pilot who goes two rounds without a Bell gets one short tip on their phone. Every round earns House Stars, which unlock paints and emblems for your Titan: looks only, never extra power.

What is not done yet

For now each Titan is a simple coloured disc on the screen; painted Titans come later. Everyone has to be in the same room: there is no way to play with friends in other homes yet. The game speaks English only.
