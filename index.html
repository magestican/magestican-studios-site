Farmyshoot

3D voxel FPS · P2P multiplayer · WASD + mouse

A browser FPS with ice-drift movement, farm-animal soldiers, seeded voxel maps, and P2P networking (no server). Capture 5 flags to win — unless the losing team can solve an anagram in 10 seconds. Cow, chicken, pig, or sheep?

Farmy Uprising

Ten-minute strategy · animals against farmers · phone, desktop and controller

Mother Nature woke the animals up and they want the farms gone. The map is cut into sectors and every second you hold one it pays you, so the game is scored in ground held rather than in kills. The Herd takes everything and holds nothing; the Yield takes it slowly and never gives it back. Both of them want the river.

Farmy Evil Hills

PS1-era survival horror · Venus · phone and desktop

Station Hesper-4 has missed three reporting cycles, and the Space Farming Agency sends one inspector because one is cheaper than two. Something airborne has been through the livestock and it does not touch humans, which is worse: Xander is not sick, he is food. Aim low — a chicken with a leg gone stops outrunning you.

Farmy Kart

3D kart racer · drift boosts · race friends by link

The Farmyshoot farm, in karts. Drift through a corner to charge a mini-turbo, throw a cowpat at whoever is winning, and hope nobody behind you draws the runaway tractor. Six circuits across two cups, eight-kart grids, and a driving model where the nose and the direction of travel are allowed to disagree.

Farmy Scrabble

The full board · 2 to 4 players · over a link

Fifteen by fifteen, the real hundred-tile bag, premium squares and the fifty-point bonus for laying all seven. Drag a tile, tap it, or type - a mouse, a thumb and a keyboard are three different hands. Send a link or read out a six-character code and up to three other people share the same board, peer to peer, with no account and no server. Every premium square carries a shape as well as a colour, and there is no clock on your turn or on the game.

Farmy Crosswords

Four word games · 223 puzzles · play together in one room

Wordle, Spelling Bee, Connections and Strands under one roof, with a farm flavour. Drag across the letters and a ribbon follows your hand; or just start typing, at any moment, without clicking into anything first. Built for eyes that are not twenty any more: nothing under 18px, nothing pressable under 48px, every colour backed by a shape, and no clock anywhere. Press Play together and somebody else joins the same puzzle - you find the words between you, and it is not a race.

The four are Farmy Wordle (65 words), Farmy Spelling Bee (53 hives), Farmy Connections (52 sets) and Farmy Strands (53 grids), and each one has a page of its own.

Farmy Chess

Complete chess · a bot or a shared link · no clock anywhere

The whole game, not a subset of it: castling on both sides, en passant, promotion to any piece, and the draws that are not stalemate. The board carries its own ranks and files, the state is written in a sentence under it, and Take a move back is on the screen rather than behind anything. There is no timer on a move, no timer on the game, and nothing counting down at the edge - which is the reason most people who stopped playing chess online stopped.

Farmy Checkers

English draughts · takes compulsory · a bot or a shared link

Twelve sheep against twelve cows. Captures are compulsory, a jump that sets up another keeps going in the same turn, and a piece that reaches the far rank is crowned. The two sides are different animals rather than two colours of the same disc, and the dark squares are hatched rather than filled - so the board reads on a phone in sunlight and reads if you cannot separate the two tones. Undo is on the screen. No clock.

Farmy Ludo

Four teams · against the computer or over a link · no clock

A six to come out, capture by landing on a single opponent, and an exact roll to get home. The four teams are Sheep, Geese, Pigs and Cows, and each one carries its own shape as well as its own colour - a circle, a triangle, a square and a diamond - because four coloured discs is the standard and it is the standard that fails in bright light. The track carries arrows, so the direction of travel is drawn rather than remembered.

2D Fighter EX

Animation experiment · seeded stage · scrub it frame by frame

EX is short for experiment. A fight animation whose project file was lost, recovered frame by frame from a screen recording — 84 frames of timing, a camera that pushes in to twice its size, and where each fighter stood — then replayed with 1990s cel-anime characters in a forest generated from a seed.

Aracstory

2D adventure · seeded multi-tile worlds · WASD

An adventure across five connected worlds, all generated from a single seed. Same seed, same world; new seed, new run. Originally started in Unity in 2013 with Nikhil V; being remade in Godot 4.

Who we are

Magestican Studios is the imprint Bryan Arbelo uses to sign small games with big procgen and hand-drawn art. The engine deals the cards from a seeded deck — a person designs every card.

No servers, no launcher, nothing to sign up to. A game is a link, and the link works on a locked-down work laptop, a Chromebook and a phone.
