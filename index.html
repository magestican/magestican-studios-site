Aracstory

2D adventure · seeded multi-tile worlds · WASD

Zelakas In Space

2D arcade shooter · three endings · keyboard

Dodge asteroids, blackholes, and drones. Three endings by how much you clear. Made in 2014 with Gonzalo and Christian in ActionScript 3; playable today in a modern browser via Ruffle, and being remade in Godot 4.

TB

Team Bondage

3D voxel FPS · P2P multiplayer · WASD + mouse

A browser FPS with ice-drift movement, farm-animal soldiers, seeded voxel maps, and P2P networking (no server). Capture 5 flags to win — unless the losing team can solve an anagram in 10 seconds. Cow, chicken, pig, or sheep?

Who we are

Magestican Studios is the imprint Bryan Arbelo uses to sign small games with big procgen and hand-drawn art. Every graphic in every game is drawn by a human hand — the engine deals the cards from a seeded deck, but a person paints every card.
