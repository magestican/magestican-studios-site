Who we are

Magestican Studios is the imprint Bryan Arbelo uses to sign small games with big procgen and hand-drawn art. The engine deals the cards from a seeded deck — a person designs every card.

No servers, no launcher, nothing to sign up to. A game is a link, and the link works on a locked-down work laptop, a Chromebook and a phone.
