Why remote teams pick Farmyshoot

  • Nothing to install — most corporate laptops block .exe and .app installs. Farmyshoot is a URL. Even the most restrictive IT policies let it through.
  • Nothing to sign up for — no email, no password. Optional Google Sign-In just remembers your name.
  • Nothing to configure — the host clicks "new game", everyone else pastes the 6-character room code.
  • No server — the game runs peer-to-peer over WebRTC. Your team's data never leaves the browsers involved.
  • Works on any device — MacBook, Windows laptop, Chromebook, iPhone, Android tablet. All the same room.
  • Bots on demand — 3 people online + a 4th about to join? Add AI bots to balance the teams while you wait.
  • Match ends fast — first-team-to-5-flags. Rounds run 5–10 minutes. Play one, get back to work; play three, break for lunch.

Recipe: a 15-minute team break

  1. Someone opens the game link, clicks Host a new game, copies the 6-character room code from the URL bar.
  2. They paste that code in your team chat.
  3. Everyone else opens the link, pastes the code, clicks Join.
  4. Pick your farm animal (cow, chicken, pig, sheep). Pick a team (red or blue).
  5. First team to 5 captures wins. If it's a tie: instant anagram tiebreaker.

Yes, that's the whole thing. No clients, no launchers, and nothing to sign up to.
