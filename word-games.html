The word games

  • Farmy Five

    Six guesses at a five-letter word, most of them off a farm. A word of the day, and every other puzzle unlocked too.

  • Farmy Hive

    Seven letters in a honeycomb, the middle one compulsory, and a pangram in every hive.

  • Farmy Herds

    Sixteen words hiding four secret groups of four. Four mistakes allowed, no timer.

  • Farmy Furrows

    A themed word search where every letter belongs to a word, plus a spangram that crosses the board.

  • Farmy Tiles

    A word-tile game on the full fifteen by fifteen board, with the real hundred-tile bag and premium squares.

    Two to four players on one board, or you against the computer. Invite by link, no account.

  • Farmy Crosswords

    Four word games on one page - Farmy Five, Farmy Hive, Farmy Herds and Farmy Furrows - with big letters and no timer.

    Up to four people working the same puzzle together. Invite by room code, no account.

Which puzzle for which mood

  • Five minutes with a coffee: Farmy Five. Six guesses at a five-letter word, most of them off a farm, with a shape as well as a colour on every tile. There are 227 Farmy Five puzzles, and a word of the day if you want the same one as everyone else.
  • A longer think: Farmy Hive. Seven letters, the middle one compulsory, words of four letters or more, and a pangram in every one of the 53 hives. The hint table counts what is left rather than what exists.
  • If you like spotting the trick: Farmy Herds. Sixteen words, four secret groups of four, four mistakes. There are 90 sets, and a wrong guess that holds three of one group is told so.
  • If you like word searches: Farmy Furrows. Every letter of the six by eight grid belongs to a themed word, and a spangram names the theme. There are 96 grids, and you earn hints by finding other words.
  • Against real people: Farmy Tiles, a crossword tile game on the full fifteen by fifteen board with the hundred-tile bag. Two to four players over a link, or you against the computer.

The first four live together in Farmy Crosswords, one page with all four on the menu.

Built for reading, not for squinting

Nothing in the word games is smaller than 18 pixels, text clears AAA contrast, and there is no timer anywhere, so nobody is rushed. That makes them a good fit for older players and for anyone playing on a phone at arm's length. Your progress lives in your own browser, with no account.

Playing with someone else

Press Play together in any of the four puzzles and you get a room with a six-character code. Up to four people can join from their own phones or computers and work the same puzzle together: it is co-operative, not a race. For a head-to-head game, Farmy Tiles is the one - send the link and take turns on one board.

Questions

Is there a daily five-letter word game here?

Yes. Farmy Five gives you six guesses at a five-letter word, with a word of the day. The other puzzles are not locked, so you can play one a day or twenty in a sitting.

Are these word games really free with no ads and no download?

Yes. They run in the browser tab, there is no account to make, nothing to install and no ads.

Can I play a word game with a friend online?

Yes. The four puzzle games have a Play together room that up to four people can share on their own devices, working one puzzle co-operatively. Farmy Tiles is the competitive one: two to four players on one board, joined from a link.
