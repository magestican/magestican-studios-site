Common English five-letter words from A to Z, a finder for words that start with, end in or contain the letters you already have, the best first guesses, and everyday words with their meaning for anyone learning English. Good first guesses

      
In a five-letter word game the first guess is a question, not an answer. Use a word with five different letters, most of them common in English (E, A, R, T, S, L, O, I), and the colours you get back will rule out or place more of the hidden word than any other start. These are good ones:

      
      
RAISE ARISE ALTER TRACE ROAST ALONE

      
      
Then make your second guess with five new letters, and only start aiming at the answer on the third.

Now guess one: Guesswise

      
Guesswise is our free five-letter word game. You get six tries to guess the hidden English word, and after each one the tiles tell you which letters are right and in the right place, which are in the word but somewhere else, and which are not in it at all. Each colour has its own shape too, so it works for colour-blind players. There are 227 puzzles and a word of the day, big letters, no timer, no account and no ads.

      
It is a good way to practise English: every guess has to be a real word, and you learn new ones from the answers. More word games for learning English.

    


    

      
Questions

      
How many five-letter words are there in English?

      
It depends on the dictionary. Guesswise accepts about sixteen thousand, many of them rare. The list on this page keeps to the common words people actually use.

      
What is a good first word in a five-letter word game?

      
One with five different, common letters, such as RAISE, ARISE, ALTER or TRACE. It tells you the most about the hidden word in one guess.

      
Does this list give away the answers to Guesswise?

      
No. Every word in the lists is one Guesswise accepts as a guess, and none of them is one of its answers.
